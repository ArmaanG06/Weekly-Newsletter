# Weekly Newsletter

A weekly options/volatility research newsletter. Each week, a Python pipeline pulls full options chains (via `yfinance`) across a basket of tickers — typically large AI-exposed names alongside broad index ETFs — and derives realized vol, IV surfaces, put skew, term structure, and IV-vs-RV spreads. Those numbers feed a short, chart-driven article (~2,000 words, written for Substack) that argues a specific thesis about what the options market is actually pricing that week, rather than just reporting where volatility indices sit.

Each week gets its own dated folder containing the supporting charts, notebook, and article draft for that edition.
